Hello! I am **Rodrigo Pérez Ortiz**, a PhD candidate in Mathematics at the [University of Bologna](https://www.unibo.it/en/homepage) and a Marie Skłodowska-Curie fellow within the [FutureData4EU](https://site.unibo.it/futuredata4eu/en) programme. Currently in LPTMS, Paris :)

My research lies at the intersection of **machine learning theory, high-dimensional statistics, and statistical physics**. I am particularly interested in understanding how the structure of data and the architecture of learning models influence learning and generalization in high-dimensional settings.

Before starting my PhD, I was a member of the **CHORAL research group**, led by Jean Barbier, at the [International Centre for Theoretical Physics (ICTP)](https://www.ictp.it/) in Trieste, Italy.

I obtained my Master's degree in **Theoretical Physics** from [École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL)](https://www.epfl.ch/en/), Switzerland, where I completed my master's thesis under the supervision of [Lenka Zdeborová](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/spoc/).

## Research Interests

My research interests include:

- Statistical physics of machine learning
- High-dimensional statistics and inference
- Theoretical machine learning
- Generalization in neural networks
- Statistical mechanics of disordered systems
- Data-driven mathematical modeling

I am particularly interested in applying tools from statistical physics and probability theory to develop a theoretical understanding of modern machine learning methods.
